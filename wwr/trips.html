Our Rafting Trips

Every trip is led by a certified guide and includes all safety gear. Pick the adventure that matches your crew, or get in touch and we'll help you choose.

Featured Trips

Colorado River Half-Day

A fast-paced introduction to white water, winding through canyon walls with splashy Class II-III rapids. Perfect for first-timers and families with older kids.

Salmon River Full-Day

A full day on one of the West's wildest rivers, with a riverside lunch included. Expect sustained Class III-IV rapids and incredible scenery.

Snake River Overnight Expedition

Two days and one night on the river, complete with riverside camping, guided hikes, and some of the biggest rapids we run. Our most popular trip for experienced groups.
